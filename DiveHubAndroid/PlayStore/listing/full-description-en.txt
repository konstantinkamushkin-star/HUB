# Google Play — full description (≤ 4000 chars)

DiveHub is the app for divers and dive centers — Android v1.2 parity with iOS.

• Map and catalog of dive sites, centers, and shops
• Logbook with search, sort, dive detail, and share to feed
• Social feed with photos, video posts, and clickable hashtags
• Friends, messaging, and global search with category filters
• Dive and course booking with full-screen confirmation
• Unified profile edit, subscription / PRO trial info, 7 languages
• Underwater photo & video editor with cloud AI (UVM)
• Statistics with icons and achievements from your logbook
• Help with searchable FAQ

For partners: dive center, instructor, and shop dashboards — courses editor with modules, trips, bookings calendar, inventory with digital checkout signature, gear, analytics, and admin web panel.

Privacy policy: https://dive-hub.ru/privacy
